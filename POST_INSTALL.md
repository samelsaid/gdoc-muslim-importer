# Quran & Hadith — Installed Successfully

Jazakallahu khairan for installing Quran & Hadith for Google Docs!

## Quick Start

1. Open any **Google Doc**
2. Click **Quran & Hadith** in the menu bar
3. Select **Open Sidebar**

## Look Up & Insert

- **Quran**: Enter a surah (1–114) and ayah number, click **Look Up** to preview, then **Insert into Doc**
- **Hadith**: Pick a collection, enter a hadith number, click **Look Up** to preview, then **Insert into Doc**

Both insert formatted blocks with Arabic text and English translation.

## Inline Tags

You can also type tags directly in your document and replace them all at once:

```
/quran 2:255
/hadith bukhari:1
```

Then click **Scan & Replace All Tags** from the sidebar or menu to convert them into formatted blocks.

## Supported Collections

Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah, Malik
